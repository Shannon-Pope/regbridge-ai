# RegBridge AI Vision

## Vision Statement

RegBridge AI transforms compliance from a late-stage product review function into a proactive market access intelligence capability.

By translating product designs, specifications, and engineering decisions into regulatory and commercialization impacts, RegBridge AI enables faster product launches, earlier design decisions, and reduced business risk across global markets. 

---

# Why We Are Building This

Today, product development teams often learn about regional compliance issues after significant engineering decisions have already been made.

Compliance experts spend substantial time manually reviewing design specifications, identifying applicable regulations, interpreting technical requirements, and communicating impacts back to engineering teams. This process is highly dependent on specialized knowledge and frequently results in multiple review cycles, delayed decisions, and late-stage design changes. 

As products become increasingly complex and regulatory requirements continue to expand, this model does not scale efficiently.

Organizations need a faster, more consistent way to evaluate product designs against market access requirements before costly downstream impacts occur.

---

# Our Vision

RegBridge AI serves as an intelligent regulatory translation layer between engineering, product planning, and regional compliance teams.

Instead of requiring compliance experts to manually interpret every design change, the platform continuously evaluates product information and produces actionable insights that support product development decisions.

The future-state workflow is:

```text
Product Design
        ↓
AI Regulatory Analysis
        ↓
Market Access Assessment
        ↓
Engineering Impact Brief
        ↓
Human Validation
        ↓
Earlier Product Decisions
        ↓
Faster Market Access
```

This approach allows regulatory expertise to be applied earlier, more consistently, and at a larger scale than traditional review processes.

---

# Strategic Outcome

RegBridge AI is not intended to be a "compliance checking tool."

It is intended to become a **Market Access Intelligence Platform** that helps product teams answer critical business questions:

- Can this product be launched in the United States?
- Can this product be launched in Canada?
- What regulatory requirements apply?
- What testing will be required?
- What engineering changes may be necessary?
- What launch risks exist?
- What is the estimated business impact of those risks? 

By providing these answers earlier in development, RegBridge AI helps reduce uncertainty and improve launch readiness.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Compliance as an Enabler

Compliance should accelerate commercialization, not act as a gate at the end of development.

RegBridge AI enables earlier visibility into requirements so product teams can make informed decisions before major investments are made.

## Human Expertise Remains Essential

The platform augments regulatory experts rather than replacing them.

AI performs analysis, identifies potential issues, summarizes regulatory requirements, and generates recommendations.

Qualified compliance professionals retain ownership of interpretation, validation, and final approval.

## Transparency and Traceability

Every recommendation should include clear justification and references to applicable regulations, standards, and approved internal guidance.

Users must understand why a recommendation was made and be able to validate the supporting evidence.

## Scalable Knowledge

Regulatory expertise should not exist only in individual subject matter experts.

RegBridge AI captures regulatory interpretations, historical decisions, and organizational knowledge in a reusable and scalable format.

# MVP Vision

The first release focuses on a narrow but high-value use case.

### Product Category

- Televisions (TVs)

### Markets

- United States
- Canada

### Regulatory Areas

- FCC
- UL
- DOE
- NRCan

### Core Capability

Using a product specification, bill of materials (BOM), and intended markets, RegBridge AI will:

1. Determine applicable regulations.
2. Identify potential compliance gaps.
3. Generate a draft Engineering Impact Brief.
4. Provide supporting rationale and confidence indicators.
5. Enable expert review before final decisions are made. 

Success in this phase establishes the foundation for broader product categories, additional regulatory domains, and deeper integration into product development processes.

# Success Definition

RegBridge AI is successful when:

- Regulatory assessments are completed in hours rather than days.
- Engineering teams receive clear, actionable guidance.
- Compliance reviews become more consistent and repeatable.
- Product teams gain earlier visibility into launch risks.
- Late-stage compliance-driven design changes are reduced.
- Compliance expertise becomes a scalable business capability rather than a bottleneck.

RegBridge AI becomes the trusted source for understanding how product decisions affect market access.
