# RegBridge AI MVP Charter

## Project Name

**RegBridge AI**
_A Market Access Intelligence Platform for Product Compliance and Regulatory Translation_

---

# Purpose

RegBridge AI is designed to accelerate product commercialization by translating product design information into actionable regulatory and engineering guidance.

The MVP will demonstrate that product specifications, bill of materials (BOM) data, and intended market information can be analyzed to identify applicable regulatory requirements, potential compliance gaps, and engineering impacts for North American market access decisions.

The objective is not to replace regulatory experts, but to reduce manual review effort, improve consistency, and provide engineering teams with earlier visibility into compliance risks.

---

# MVP Objectives

The MVP will validate the following capabilities:

1. Identify applicable regulations based on product characteristics.
2. Assess product information against defined regulatory requirements.
3. Generate a preliminary compliance gap assessment.
4. Translate regulatory findings into engineering-focused recommendations.
5. Produce a standardized Engineering Impact Brief for human review.

---

# Scope

## Product Scope

The MVP is limited to:

- Televisions (TVs)

Products outside the TV category are excluded from the MVP and will be considered in future phases.

---

## Geographic Scope

The MVP supports the following markets:

- United States
- Canada

Regulatory requirements from other regions are out of scope.

---

## Regulatory Scope

The MVP will focus on the following regulatory domains:

### United States

- Federal Communications Commission (FCC)
- Department of Energy (DOE)
- UL Safety Requirements

### Canada

- Natural Resources Canada (NRCan)

Additional regulatory areas such as environmental compliance, recycling obligations, cybersecurity requirements, accessibility requirements, and state/provincial regulations are excluded from the MVP.

---

# Inputs

The MVP will analyze the following inputs:

## Product Specification

Examples include:

- Product description
- Technical specifications
- Product features
- Product architecture
- Power configuration
- Communications capabilities

## Bill of Materials (BOM)

Examples include:

- Major electrical components
- Power supply information
- Wireless modules
- Safety-critical components

## Intended Markets

Examples include:

- United States
- Canada
- U.S. and Canada combined launch

---

# Outputs

The MVP will generate the following outputs.

## 1. Regulatory Applicability Assessment

A determination of which regulations apply to the product based on product characteristics and market selection.

Example outputs:

- FCC Applicable
- DOE Applicable
- UL Applicable
- NRCan Applicable

---

## 2. Regulatory Gap Analysis

A preliminary assessment of potential compliance issues, including:

- Passed areas
- Areas requiring review
- Potential gaps
- Missing information
- Regulatory uncertainties

---

## 3. Engineering Impact Brief

A standardized summary intended for engineering teams that includes:

- Product overview
- Applicable markets
- Applicable regulations
- Design impacts
- Required testing
- Documentation requirements
- Open questions
- Recommended actions
- Risk assessment
- Confidence score

---

# Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful if it can:

- Consistently determine applicable regulations for TV products.
- Produce repeatable applicability assessments using defined product inputs.
- Generate a draft Engineering Impact Brief requiring only expert validation.
- Demonstrate meaningful reduction in manual first-pass compliance review effort.
- Provide traceability from recommendations back to regulatory requirements and internal guidance.

---

# Out of Scope

The following items are not included in the MVP:

- Automated regulatory monitoring
- Environmental regulations (RoHS, REACH, PFAS, EPR, battery laws)
- Product categories other than TVs
- Direct PLM integrations
- Automated approval workflows
- Risk prediction using machine learning
- Global regulatory coverage beyond the U.S. and Canada
- Fully autonomous compliance decisions

---

# Governance

RegBridge AI serves as a decision-support tool.

All regulatory assessments, gap analyses, and engineering recommendations generated by the system must be reviewed and validated by qualified compliance personnel before being communicated to engineering teams or used in product launch decisions.

# Vision Statement

RegBridge AI will evolve from a compliance assessment tool into a Market Access Intelligence Platform that enables faster product launches, earlier engineering decisions, and reduced commercialization risk by translating regulatory requirements into actionable product guidance.
